Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India | Former President

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs, earning the title "Missile Man of India".

Achievements

  • 11th President of India (2002–2007)
  • Key role in Pokhran-II nuclear tests
  • Developed India's missile technology
  • Recipient of Bharat Ratna

Famous Quote

"Dream is not that which you see while sleeping, it is something that does not let you sleep."
